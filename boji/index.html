拍照认食物Snap to recognise

对着饭菜拍一张，自动认出是什么，并估算千焦。Take a photo of your meal. It recognises the food and estimates kJ.

✨

千焦标在食物上kJ right on your food

用 AR 把千焦数字直接标在每样食物旁边。AR places the numbers right next to each item on your plate.

经你同意后读取健康数据，每天给你一条贴心建议。With your permission, it reads Health data to give you a daily tip.

千焦是估算值，建议仅供参考，不能代替医生或营养师。Energy values are estimates. Tips are for reference only and are not medical advice.
